    Evaluation rubric

    Completeness (1–5)

    Does the atom set preserve all affective interpretations (mood/emotion/aesthetic) in the representation? Don't penalize missing plain visual facts that aren't themselves vibe interpretations.

    • 5 — All affective interpretations preserved
    • 4 — Nearly all, minor omissions
    • 3 — Several missing, overall meaning mostly retained
    • 2 — Many important ones omitted
    • 1 — Most affective meaning lost

    Atom Quality (per atom, auto-aggregated)

    Rate each atom on 4 criteria. An atom is Good only if all 4 hold; otherwise Bad. The atom-quality score is (# Good / total) × 5, computed automatically.

    • Affectiveness — expresses a perceived mood/atmosphere/aesthetic, not just an objective visual fact.
    • Atomicity — expresses a single independently evaluable affective interpretation (closely related/synonymous vibes may be grouped).
    • Fidelity — faithfully decomposes the original; doesn't paraphrase, add new reasoning, or introduce information not explicitly expressed in the original.
    • Evidence preservation — if the original ties the vibe to specific visual evidence, that evidence is still attached in the atom. Automatically Bad if Fidelity is Bad.
