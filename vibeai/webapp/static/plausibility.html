    Evaluation rubric

    Plausibility (per atom)

    Each atom is one of two types:

    • vibe_only — a standalone vibe claim with no stated evidence (e.g. "The vibe is relaxed."). Judge whether the stated vibe can plausibly be inferred from the image.
    • evidence_backed — a claim that names specific visual evidence and links it to a vibe (e.g. "Bright sunlight glints off the water, suggesting a breezy afternoon."). Judge whether: (1) the stated evidence is actually present in the image, (2) the stated vibe can plausibly be inferred from the image, and (3) the mapping from the stated evidence to the stated vibe is reasonable.

    Mark an atom Plausible only if it holds up on all applicable points above; otherwise mark Not plausible. A reason is optional.
